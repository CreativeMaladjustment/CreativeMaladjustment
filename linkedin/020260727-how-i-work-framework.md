# A How-I-Work Framework

**Date:** 02026-07-27  
**Original Post:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/creativemaladjustment_how-i-work-framework-activity-7485344514442797057-KfS-)

I asked my wife (Emily Webster, M.S.) what I should work on. I wanted a real challenge, and she knows me well enough to give one.

Her spark of genius and answer was five words: "a how-I-work framework."

And just like that, the challenge I asked for turned into a project I am excited to work on.

Here's why it clicked. For almost five years I've experienced a culture of learning at Guild, where the mission is unlocking opportunity and helping people skill up. I've watched and learned as we do that for our members.

So that's the project: not skilling up to become something new, but maturing skills I already have: how I show up at work. Getting better at it while staying true to who I am.

I'm learning from the best teacher I know, my dad (Emmett Davis), who teaches frameworks to his students. Here's the first thing I learned talking to him: information isn't behavior. Tell two people it's 32°F and one stays inside while the other grabs a coat and goes for a run, same fact, different framework. Most of the time when someone reacts differently than you expected, they didn't mishear you. They just ran your information through a different framework than yours.

Co-leading one of our ERGs provided early access to a direct investment from Guild in my development, including a coach in my corner. Nicole López-Conti, MA, PCC has been a real source of encouragement and a whole additional well of experience to draw from, and part of why I want to share what I'm building.

Vulnerable part? Writing down a "How I Work" framework is me making it visible to myself and those I share it with. And I'm still figuring out what my answers are. What's attached is the blueprint: the foundation I'm putting together. The personal pieces (how I work) come next, and I'll share them as I go.

Do you have a framework for turning information into how you actually act? I'd love to know if this is useful to others.
