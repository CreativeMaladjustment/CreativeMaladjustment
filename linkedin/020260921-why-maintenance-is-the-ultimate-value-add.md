# Why Maintenance is the Ultimate Value Add

**Date:** 02026-09-21
**Original Post:** [LinkedIn](https://lnkd.in/p/gjJw_HHP)

We live in a culture that celebrates the "new," but I believe maintenance needs to be celebrated again. In my framework, maintenance is never wasted effort: it is the foundation of trust and long-term viability. I grew up near Minneapolis and frequently crossed the I-35 bridge. My Aunt worked in an office with a direct view of that bridge. Instead of watching the slow, difficult work of preventative maintenance, she watched from her window as the wreckage was cleaned up after the bridge collapsed. It was a visceral lesson: if you do not pay for maintenance now, you will pay much more to replace the system later.

The 1:16 Ratio I have adopted a concept in my professional life borrowed from medical preventative care. One unit of effort spent on "brushing the teeth" of a system must save sixteen units of effort required for an emergency "extraction". Whether it is managing forests to prevent fires or refining software code to avoid a crash, the essential, behind-the-scenes work is what keeps us safe and profitable.

I view my role not just as "doing the work," but as understanding the work so I can improve its speed, quality, and resilience. I want to be a partner who helps you "brush the teeth" of your processes before they become painful.

What is the foundational maintenance task in your world that deserves more credit? Let’s talk about how we can build a culture that rewards reliability as much as innovation.
